Common Questions

How do I unlock Glass Cockpit?

Open the Shop tab in the app and start the free trial. Glass Cockpit is an auto-renewing monthly subscription managed entirely through your Apple ID — Chair Fly never sees your payment details.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Chair Fly, and manage or cancel it there. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access through the end of the period you already paid for.

I bought Glass Cockpit on another device — how do I restore it?

Open the Shop tab and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

The app isn't calibrating level correctly. What do I do?

Set the iPad flat on a table (or however you plan to hold it) and tap Calibrate on the panel you're using. That resets whatever position you're holding it in as level flight. If it still looks off, email us — we're happy to help track it down.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

Email info@chairflytrainer.com with what you were doing and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help a lot.

Is Chair Fly authorized for real-world navigation?

No. Chair Fly is a training aid only and is not authorized for actual navigation. See the Disclaimer for details.
